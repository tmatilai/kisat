# Code of conduct

Kisat follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).

## Reporting an issue

Report a breach by email to the address under "Contact" in the [README](README.md#contact). The
maintainer reads the report, keeps it private and answers it.

## Addressing and repairing harm

The maintainer follows the Covenant's own section of this name, with its steps as written.
